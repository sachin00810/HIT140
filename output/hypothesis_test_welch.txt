HYPOTHESIS TEST — WELCH INDEPENDENT SAMPLES t-TEST
==============================================================

Research question
-----------------
  Is there a significant difference in the average number of corners
  won by teams that win their match compared with teams that lose?

Hypotheses
----------
  H0 : mu_win  = mu_loss
  Ha : mu_win != mu_loss    (two-sided)

Why Welch
---------
  equal_var=False, so equal population variances are not assumed.
  This is the safer default and costs almost nothing in power.

Group summaries
---------------
  Winning teams    n =  60, mean = 5.3333, sd = 3.1712
  Losing teams     n =  60, mean = 3.8500, sd = 2.8808

Test results
------------
  mean difference    : 1.4833
  95% CI for diff    : [0.3879, 2.5787]
  t statistic        : 2.6818
  degrees of freedom : 116.93
  p value            : 0.008382
  Cohen's d          : 0.4896
  alpha              : 0.05
  DECISION           : REJECT H0
